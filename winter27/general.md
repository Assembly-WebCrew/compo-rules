# General rules

These general rules apply to all competitions. Compo-specific rules override them where applicable. If in doubt, contact CompoCrew at <compo-contact@assembly.org> or on the #demoscene channel of the Assembly Discord server. 

- Entries are submitted at [scene.assembly.org](https://scene.assembly.org/), which is published approximately two weeks before the event. CompoCrew announces updates about the competitions on that platform and you can also find the [full schedule of deadlines and juries](https://scene.assembly.org/event/timetable/). All deadlines are hard: make sure to submit your entry, provide the required information and upload the files in time before the deadline - contact the organizer if you're stuck.
- Whether remote entries are allowed depends on the specific competition's rules. A ticket is not required for a remote entry but is required to vote.
- By default, desktop entries run on the compo PC. If your entry needs a desktop platform we do not provide, for example macOS, a specific GPU, or a specific Linux setup, you may bring your own machine and we will capture the entry from it, provided you arrange this with the compo crew before the deadline and the machine outputs 1080p60 over HDMI.
- A valid IBAN is required to receive money prizes. Money prizes are paid afterwards, so you do not need to be present at the party. Only a physical trophy requires the author or a designated proxy to be present at the prize giving.
- Submitting an entry means accepting the competition contract.
- Maximum one entry per artist per competition. If you submit multiple entries, only one will be accepted.
- Every entry and its main creative effort must be previously unreleased. Realtime entries may include music and graphics that compete at the same event. Previously released components, such as fonts, system samples, and licensed libraries, are allowed only when they are not the main effort and you have the rights to use them. After an entry has been shown in the competition, you may release or exhibit it.
- AI-generated content is not allowed unless the competition's rules explicitly permit it. This applies to the entry's own content: code, graphics, and music. Tools used to make the entry, such as synths, trackers, packers, editors, and compilers, are fine even if AI was used to develop those tools.
- You must own or have permission for all material in your entry. The burden of proof is on you. Trademarked or copyrighted content, including logos, is not allowed without permission. Finnish copyright law has no "fair use". See [On Copyright](./copyright.md) for what this means in practice.
- Fan art using copyrighted characters or works is not allowed. With money prizes at stake, this is enforced strictly.
- Every competition has a time slot, which determines how many entries can be shown. If more entries are submitted than fit, a preselection jury chooses which entries are shown.
- Entries that are not shown are not released by Assembly, so they are not counted as released and may be entered at another party.
- Organizers may disqualify explicit, racist, or disturbing material, modify rules to clarify ambiguities, or change prizes, especially if entry quality or number is low.

## Capture assistance

Entries are shown on stage from recordings. Entries that run on the compo PC are captured by the compo crew as part of the normal competition process.

If your entry runs on anything else - your own machine, a console, retro hardware, or another platform - you are primarily expected to record the entry yourself and submit the video. We can help with capturing at CompoBase, but this is assistance rather than a guaranteed service. Agree on it with the compo crew well before the deadline and state in the submission notes what your entry needs to run, including the platform, browser, cables, and audio output. If the device has no external audio output, deliver the soundtrack separately.

## Compobase

During the event, if you wish to or need to meet CompoCrew in person at Compobase at the event venue - while you could pay a visit spontaneously during some reasonable time of the day/afternoon/evening if a member of the team is present there to assist you, however it's advised if possible, to ask CompoCrew in one of the above mentioned communication channels beforehand for a suitable time to meet for your matter in question as we may be not available help you in your case yet in the immediate moment.
