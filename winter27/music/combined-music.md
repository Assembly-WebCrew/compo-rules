# Combined Music

Refer to the [General rules](../general.md) with the following additions:

Make the track you have always wanted to make using the platform and technique of your choice.

- Your entry must be your own work. Covers, remixes, and rearrangements are not allowed. Copyrighted samples and other copyrighted material are not allowed unless you have a licence covering this use, such as a Creative Commons licence or sample-library licence.
- If you use Creative Commons or Public Domain material, declare it in the entry form and cite the source.
- AI may not be used to compose or generate the music. A track or part of a track produced by prompting is not allowed. Using AI as part of the production process, such as an AI-generated vocal sound for your own lyrics and composition, is acceptable if you have the rights to the generated material and follow the other rules. Tools such as synths, trackers, DAWs, and effects are fine even if AI was used to develop them.
- You may not use music for which you have not acquired the necessary rights. Most published music is controlled by Teosto or Gramex and is therefore not usable. You are liable for infringements.
- There are no restrictions on composing techniques. Modern DAWs, trackers, oldskool platforms, and other methods are allowed.
- Supported delivery formats are 320 kbps MP3, FLAC, and WAV.
- Entries made on oldskool platforms or in tracker formats must also be converted and submitted in one of the supported delivery formats.
- A preselection jury will select entries to fit the competition time slot, typically 45 minutes. Entries that are not shown are handled as described in the general rules.
- For playback, all entries are converted to FLAC and ReplayGain volume control is applied. Normalising the volume is recommended; excessive compression gives no advantage.
- The entry must be no longer than 3 minutes 30 seconds. Exceeding the limit results in disqualification rather than a fade-out.
- Entries are presented anonymously until voting has closed. Do not put an artist or group name in the title, filename, technical details, or submission notes.

Remote entries are welcome.
