# Wild (with AI)

Refer to the [General rules](../general.md) with the following additions:

- This competition is for audiovisual productions that do not fit into the other competitions, including realtime productions that use AI-generated creative assets. The finished entry must be submitted as a 1080p video. Standalone images and audio-only works are not eligible.
- AI-generated material may be used, but the finished entry must include substantial creative work by the participating artist or group.
- A single AI-generated video clip submitted as the finished work is not eligible. Combining generated outputs with only basic trimming, sequencing, transitions, or other minor edits is also not sufficient.
- The entrant must shape the production through meaningful creative work such as editing, compositing, animation, narrative, programming, performance, or sound design. Prompting alone is not a sufficient creative contribution.
- Fan art and other uses of copyrighted or trademarked material are not allowed. You must own or have permission to use all AI-generated and other material in the entry. If asked, you must show where the material came from and what allows you to use it.
- If generative AI is used directly to create the entry, enable the **Gen AI** option in the entry form.
- Submit a `readme.txt` identifying the generative AI methods used and briefly describing both their use and the entrant's own creative contribution. If no generative AI was used, state that in the readme.
- Using a production tool developed with generative AI does not by itself require the **Gen AI** option. Enable it only when generative AI was used directly to create the submitted entry.
- Entries may not exceed 5 minutes in length.
- Submit a screenshot representing the entry.
- If the entry is intended to run on the compo machine, include the original files needed to run the production and provide clear instructions for running it.

Remote entries are welcome.
