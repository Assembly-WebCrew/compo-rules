# Wild (with AI)

Refer to the [General rules](../general.md) with the following additions:

- Entries can be anything that does not fit into the other competitions.
- Entries may contain AI-generated material or be generated entirely with AI.
- Fan art and other uses of copyrighted or trademarked material are not allowed. You must have the rights to all material in the entry.
- Submit a `readme.txt` describing the AI tools used and how AI was used. State explicitly if no AI was used.
- Entries may not exceed 5 minutes in length.
- Submit a screenshot representing the entry.
- Every entry must include a 1080p video capture, which will be used for presentation. See Capture assistance in the general rules.
- Entries should also be presentable using the provided compo machines where applicable.
- If the entry is executable, also submit the executable and the instructions and files needed to run it. It must show in a similar manner each time it is executed.
- The submission size must be reasonable. It may be delivered through Partyman or on a USB stick by prior arrangement with the compo crew.

Remote entries are welcome.
