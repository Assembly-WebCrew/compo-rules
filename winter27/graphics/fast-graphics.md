# Fast Graphics

Refer to the [General rules](../general.md) with the following additions:

- All entries must be original. No covers, copies, or reproductions of someone else's work.
- If you use Creative Commons or Public Domain material, declare it in the entry form and cite the source.
- AI-generated content is not allowed. The entry's content must be human-made; tools are covered by the distinction in the general rules.
- Fan art using copyrighted characters or works is not allowed.
- Submit the image as an RGB PNG or JPEG. CMYK images are not supported.
- The image resolution must be 1920x1080.
- Submit a version of the final image with no visible signature, using `_nosig` in its filename. Entries without an unsigned version will be disqualified.
- You have 1.5 hours to complete the picture and must submit it by the competition deadline.
- The picture must follow the theme and contain a specific graphical element. Both will be announced in the Partyman news at [scene.assembly.org](https://scene.assembly.org/) at the start of the 1.5-hour competition period.
- No intermediate stages are required.
- Clear rule violations result in disqualification; resubmission will not be requested.
- Entries are presented anonymously until voting has closed. Do not put an artist or group name in the title, filename, technical details, or submission notes.

Remote entries are welcome.
