# Photo

Refer to the [General rules](../general.md) with the following additions:

- Submit the displayed image as an RGB PNG or JPEG at exactly 1920x1080. CMYK images and portrait orientation are not supported.
- Only minor retouching and adjustments are allowed:
  - cropping and straightening
  - lens correction and scaling
  - color-temperature and exposure adjustment
  - desaturation
- Do not add or remove elements from the picture.
- Composite and multiple-exposure photographs are not allowed. The photo must be taken with a single exposure; this excludes internal photo stacking and HDR.
- Automatic non-generative camera processing is allowed only when it does not combine multiple exposures or generate, add, or remove image content.
- AI-generated content is not allowed. Generative filling, replacement, and other generated image content are prohibited.
- Submit the photo without a watermark or visible signature and include `_nosig` in the filename.
- If the displayed photo has been adjusted, also submit the untouched original at its native resolution. Camera-original formats, including RAW, DNG, and HEIC, are accepted, as are JPG and PNG.
- Original files and optional behind-the-scenes material are used only for verification and will not be distributed.
- For a scan of a photograph or slide, retain the physical original and provide evidence or present it to the jury if requested.
- Entries are presented anonymously until voting has closed. Do not put an artist or group name in the image, filename, title, technical details, or submission notes.
- Clear rule violations result in disqualification; resubmission will not be requested.

Remote entries are welcome.
