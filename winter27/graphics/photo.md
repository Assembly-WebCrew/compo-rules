# Photo

Refer to the [General rules](../general.md) with the following additions:

- Theme: "Shine bright in the darkness"
- All entries must be original and unreleased. No covers, copies, or reproductions of someone else’s work.
- AI-generated content is not allowed in this compo. Tools you use to make the entry (such as Lightroom, Photoshop, Gimp, and other editors etc.) are fine even if AI was used to develop those tools – using them does not make your entry AI-generated. However using generative fill with prompted AI is not allowed in any tools. 
- Fan art using copyrighted characters or works is not allowed.
- The only supported image formats are PNG and JPG. Only RGB colour space allowed – no CMYK images.
- PNG or JPEG image will be the primary submission and will be shown on the big screen.
- Pictures will be shown in 1920×1080 resolution with full 24-bit color depth.
- Submit a version of the final image with NO visible signature in the .ZIP file. Name this file with “_nosig” prefix (e.g. “cool_image.png” for the final image and “cool_image_nosig.png” for unsigned version of the same image). Entries without an unsigned version will be disqualified.
- A text file named “readme.txt” stating used techniques and sources of any material used in the entry must be submitted.
- Any photographing technique is welcome. Digital, film and any combination of these all belong in this compo.
- Multiple exposure is allowed. 
- The image resolution must be 1920×1080. Feel free to make the image 1080×1920, but due to the letterboxing, it will appear smaller on screen and in the stream as well.
- You must submit clear evidence of the process of creating the photo competition picture in the form of at least THREE versions of the unfinished picture that show the creation process, if it has been drastically edited. Attach the original raw photo in original resolution converted to JPG. You can also attach ”Behind the scenes” photos to the zip file, but they are not required. This means that the different steps of the process (e.g. original photo, retouching, layers) are shown in different files from different points in time when the picture is being created.
- Original files and optional bts / proof of work are only used for verification and will not be distributed. 
- Order the unfinished stages of the picture by renaming the files with appropriate prefixes (f.i. “stage1.png”, “stage2.png”) and put them into a subdirectory called “unfinished” in the ZIP archive.
- Write the techniques used in the submission form (Partyman) while submitting the image, maximum of 5 words.
- Clear rule violations will result in disqualification – we do not request resubmissions.
- Entries are presented anonymously: the artist or group name is not shown on screen or in Partyman until voting has closed. Please don’t put your name in the entry title or the file name. This includes your slide texts: do not write anything in the title, technical details, or submission notes that reveals who made the entry.

Remote entries are welcome.
